Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

At the Chhatrapati Shivaji Institute of Technology (CSIT) Durg, we hold a deep commitment to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and security of student records and counseling data. This Privacy Policy details how the CSIT Mentor Diary web application collects, secures, processes, and restricts access to academic and personal information.

1. Institutional Data Principles

CSIT Mentor Diary is constructed as a secure, closed institutional hub. We do not license, sell, or trade student records or faculty entries to third-party commercial entities. All data hosted in this platform remains the sole property of Chhatrapati Shivaji Institute of Technology and is processed exclusively for academic monitoring, mentorship guidance, and internal administrative reporting.

2. Information We Collect

To enable structured mentorship tracking, the system processes and archives the following categories of data:

  • Student Credentials & Demographics: Full name, university enrollment number, department, semester, photograph, residential address, and parent/guardian contact details.
  • Academic Metrics: Marks obtained in class tests, end-semester examinations, project reviews, laboratory marks, and cumulative attendance statistics.
  • Mentorship Logs & Notes: Written records submitted by designated faculty mentors covering behavioral counseling, career guidance, skill improvements, and critical interventions.
  • System Access Logs: IP addresses, browser specifications, login/logout timestamps, and resource actions to preserve a chronological audit log of all data modifications.
Important Security Note: All mentorship logs and counseling notes are classified as strictly confidential and are shielded under institutional guidelines to protect student-faculty trust.

3. Authentication & Access Boundaries (RBAC)

We enforce a strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) model to partition data visibility and security. The system operates on standard secure principles, ensuring users have access only to information necessary for their roles:

  • Administrators: Full access to system configuration, user provisioning, audit logs, and global department charts.
  • Mentors: Read and write permissions restricted exclusively to the list of students assigned to their mentorship batches. Mentors cannot inspect counseling logs of other departments or unassigned batches.
  • Students: Read-only access to their own academic cards, profile data, and general notices. Students cannot view other students' records or mentor-only archival notes.

4. Security Standards & Data Protection

The CSIT Mentor Diary platform employs a series of robust technical measures to safeguard information:

  • Secure Tokens: Authentication is handled via cryptographically signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT) stored temporarily in safe session memory.
  • Transit Encryption: All communications between the browser client and the application servers are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).
  • Database Protection: Database storage is shielded by access control credentials and isolated within secure local networks or cloud VPC configurations.

5. Data Retention & Archival

Student records and mentor interaction histories are retained for the duration of the student's enrollment at CSIT Durg, plus a mandatory post-graduation institutional archival period. This ensures compliance with regulatory university audits and accreditation frameworks. Once the retention criteria are satisfied, records are securely archived or purged from active systems.

CSIT Durg reserves the right to modify this Privacy Policy to align with new institutional protocols or technological updates. Users will be notified of major policy changes. For questions or support regarding data rights, please contact our support team:
